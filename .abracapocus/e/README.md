# e

## Goal

Implement motion, character, diffusion, and stereo processing, completing the binding repeat-chain order inside DelayVoice. Source/DSP/ModulationEngine.h/.cpp: per-voice per-channel sinusoidal modulation m = A_effective * sin(2*pi*rate*n/Fs + phase), rate range 0.05-5 Hz, user depth range 0-8 ms, right-channel phase = left + phaseOffset with offset range 0-180 degrees; the rack-character pitch cap: peak rate deviation = 2*pi*rateHz*depthSeconds, capped at maxRateDeviation = 2^(25/1200) - 1 (approximately 0.01455, i.e. 25 cents), so maxDepthForRate = maxRateDeviation/(2*pi*rateHz) and depthEffective = min(depthUser, maxDepthForRate) - deeper movement is available at slow rates, extreme wobble is prevented at fast rates, and Bend mode is exempt from this cap; random drift from bounded white noise in [-1,1] low-passed at 0.08 Hz cutoff via drift[n] = alphaDrift*drift[n-1] + (1-alphaDrift)*noise[n], combined m_total = m_LFO + driftAmount*drift, with the combined result clamped to the valid delay range. Source/DSP/SoftSaturator.h/.cpp: s_D(x) = tanh(D*x)/D with unity small-signal slope, drive D = 1 + 4*s for control s in [0,1], blend y = (1-s)*x + s*s_D(x); base-rate processing, no oversampling in 1.0, conservative drive, placed after LPF/guard/softening and before diffusion; small-signal loop gain must not exceed unity. Source/DSP/ShortDiffuser.h/.cpp: three first-order allpass stages per channel, H(z) = (-a + z^-M)/(1 - a*z^-M), difference equation y[n] = -a*x[n] + x[n-M] + a*y[n-M]; left delays 3.1/4.7/6.9 ms, right delays 3.7/5.3/7.9 ms, coefficients 0.45/0.38/0.32; user control d in [0, 0.40] where the 0.40 cap applies to the normalized blend before the equal-power law z_diff = cos(pi*d/2)*z + sin(pi*d/2)*allpass(z); bounded output, no independent late-reverb tail. Source/DSP/StereoProcessor.h/.cpp: constant-power pan gain_L = cos(pi*(p+1)/4), gain_R = sin(pi*(p+1)/4) for p in [-1,1]; mid/side width M=(L+R)/sqrt2, S=(L-R)/sqrt2, L_out=(M+w*S)/sqrt2, R_out=(M-w*S)/sqrt2 for w in [0,2]; low-frequency mono splitting each wet channel at the 60-500 Hz crossover (default 180 Hz), M_low=(L_low+R_low)/2, output M_low + high bands, dry path unaffected; correlation telemetry from smoothed sumLR/sumL2/sumR2 as correlation = sumLR/sqrt(sumL2*sumR2 + epsilon) clamped to [-1,1] for display. Integration completes the DelayVoice chain: saturation then diffusion after the softener, then the feedback tap. tests/MotionStereoTests.cpp: requested LFO rate and stereo phase; continuity and bounded delay positions; the 25-cent cap enforced at fast rates with deeper depth honored at slow rates; saturator transparent at zero, unity small-signal slope, finite output; diffuser bounded, near-unity magnitude, 0.40 cap honored; M/S width math exact at w=1; low-frequency mono behavior below and above crossover.

## Notes

- Async: Wave 1: e.1 (ModulationEngine), e.2 (SoftSaturator), e.3 (ShortDiffuser), and e.4 (StereoProcessor) run in parallel with disjoint files. Wave 2: e.5 integrates saturation and diffusion into DelayVoice.h/.cpp completing the binding order and registers new sources in CMakeLists.txt (e.5 depends on e.1, e.2, e.3, e.4). Final wave: e.6 creates tests/MotionStereoTests.cpp and registers it in CMakeLists.txt (e.6 depends on e.5).
- Risk: The 25-cent modulation cap is the difference between polished rack movement and chorus artifacts; it must be enforced in the engine, not documented as advice. Diffusion beyond three short stages or the 0.40 cap turns the product into a bad reverb and is forbidden.
